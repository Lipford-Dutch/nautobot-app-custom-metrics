Why it wins

No more SIM spreadsheets. No more mystery modems.

Device-native

Cellular state extends Nautobot `Device`, `Interface`, and `InventoryItem` instead of inventing parallel inventory.

Conflict-aware

Observed SIMs disagreeing with desired assignments are surfaced as operator decisions, not silently accepted drift.

Telemetry sane

Nautobot keeps latest normalized state while Prometheus and Splunk handle high-frequency history and logs.

DiffSync ready

Vendor payloads are normalized through Pydantic before reconciliation ever touches the database.
